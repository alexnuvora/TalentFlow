# TalentFlow Recruitment Partner Agreement — operating template

> Operational template for legal review before signature. Complete the bracketed commercial/legal details before onboarding a live partner.

## Parties
**TalentFlow recruitment business:** [legal entity name, company number, registered address] ("TalentFlow")  
**Recruitment Partner:** [full legal name / business name, address, Pakistan tax/registration details where applicable] ("Partner")

## Appointment and status
The Partner is appointed on a non-exclusive independent-contractor basis to identify prospective hiring clients, source candidates and support recruitment assignments allocated through TalentFlow. Nothing in this agreement creates employment, worker status, partnership, agency authority to bind TalentFlow, or authority to agree fees/terms with a hirer unless TalentFlow gives written authority.

The practical working arrangement must remain consistent with independent-contractor status. The Partner controls when and where they perform the services, subject to agreed deadlines, confidentiality, security and client/candidate requirements.

## Commission
Commission is [30]% of the net recruitment fee actually received and retained by TalentFlow for a placement attributable to the Partner, excluding VAT, refunds, rebates, chargebacks and pass-through costs.

No commission becomes payable until TalentFlow has received cleared client funds. If a fee is refunded or reduced under a client rebate/replacement arrangement before commission is paid, commission is calculated on the amount retained. If commission has already been paid, any clawback must follow the expressly agreed schedule: [insert schedule].

The Partner cannot charge candidates or work-seekers any fee for work-finding services.

## Client terms and authority
The Partner may prospect for potential clients but must not bind TalentFlow, quote non-approved commercial terms, sign a client contract, make warranties, or represent that a vacancy is confirmed unless TalentFlow has recorded the hirer's instruction.

TalentFlow controls final client terms, pricing, invoicing and collection.

## Candidate handling
The Partner must use candidate information only for authorised TalentFlow recruitment purposes and only for vacancies or talent-pool activity assigned to them. Candidate information must not be copied to personal drives, private email, messaging apps, spreadsheets or another ATS unless TalentFlow has expressly authorised the transfer.

Identifiable candidate information may be submitted to a prospective employer only through the authorised TalentFlow workflow and where the applicable recruitment business has a lawful basis and authority to make that submission.

## Confidentiality and security
The Partner must:
- keep credentials private and use MFA where available;
- access TalentFlow only from devices/accounts under their control;
- immediately report suspected loss, unauthorised access, phishing, disclosure or credential compromise;
- not bulk-export candidate/client information;
- not share CV links or candidate records outside the authorised recruitment purpose;
- comply with TalentFlow security instructions and access reviews; and
- permanently delete local copies when instructed or when the engagement ends.

TalentFlow may suspend or revoke access immediately to protect candidates, clients or the service.

## Data protection and Pakistan access
Before the Partner can access live candidate personal information from Pakistan, the parties must document their data-protection roles and execute the required data-processing/data-sharing terms.

Where the arrangement is a restricted transfer under UK GDPR, TalentFlow must record the applicable transfer mechanism before access is enabled. Where appropriate safeguards are used, the required UK data protection test / transfer risk assessment must be completed and any additional technical, organisational or contractual protections implemented.

The Partner must process personal information only on documented instructions where acting as a processor, assist with rights/security obligations as agreed, notify TalentFlow of incidents without undue delay, and return/delete personal information at termination.

## AI and recruitment decisions
AI-generated screening, summaries and scores are decision support. The Partner must review source evidence and must not make a final hiring/rejection decision solely because of an AI score or output. Protected characteristics must not be inferred or used unlawfully.

## Ownership and non-circumvention
TalentFlow retains its platform, brand, systems, client contracts and confidential business information. Candidate personal information is not owned by either party and remains subject to data-protection law.

During the engagement and for [6] months after it ends, the Partner must not deliberately circumvent TalentFlow to collect directly a recruitment fee arising from a client introduction or active assignment first introduced through TalentFlow, subject to applicable law and legal review.

## Records
The Partner must keep recruitment activity in TalentFlow so the business can evidence client instructions, candidate authority, screening/review, submissions, interviews, outcomes and commission attribution.

## Termination
Either party may terminate on [7] days' written notice. TalentFlow may suspend or terminate immediately for confidentiality, security, data-protection, fraud, candidate-charging, client misrepresentation or serious conduct concerns. System access ends on termination.

Accrued commission on qualifying placements remains governed by the commission and rebate terms above.

## Governing law
This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales and the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction, subject to legal review of enforceability and any mandatory rules applying to the Partner in Pakistan.
